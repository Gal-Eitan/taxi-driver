There are no games with the main genre "Adventure", the sub-genres "Driving" and "Wargame", and the tag "War"¹.

Out of the games released on Steam with the main genre "Indie", the sub-genres "Driving" and "Wargame", and the tag "War"²:

- **Games matching filters:** 12 / 134.1K (0%)
    
- **Total revenue:** $876.6K / $102.2B (0%)
    
- **Median revenue:** $300
    
- **Average price:** $7.00
    
- **Average playtime:** 8.1 hours
    

These results suggest that our game will enter a small niche with relatively little direct competition, but also uncertain demand.

## 2. Target Audience

The target audience for our game is people aged 12 or 16 and older, depending on the amount of violence and blood included.

The game will primarily appeal to players who enjoy action, driving challenges, and games set during wartime.

## 3. Competitor Analysis

An analysis of the five top games on Gamealytic³ reveals that most focus on the experience of combatants in war or military simulations. They also emphasize military vehicles rather than civilian ones.

Their average price is $11.19.

## 4. Unique Selling Proposition

The overwhelming majority of games we examined focus on military experiences. Our game will instead focus on the story of an ordinary civilian caught in the middle of a war.

This perspective will distinguish our game from competitors while combining driving challenges with a narrative about survival.

## 5. Business Model and Monetization

Our game will use a one-time purchase model.

We believe this model is appropriate because advertisements and microtransactions could interrupt the gameplay and undermine the atmosphere and story.

## 6. Advertising and Distribution Strategy

Our advertising strategy will focus on social media, using gameplay trailers, teasers, and development logs to attract potential players.

For distribution, Steam and itch.io will serve PC players, while Google Play will be considered for a mobile release.

## 7. LiveOps

Since our game is primarily a single-player experience, extensive LiveOps will not be necessary.

However, occasional updates could introduce new levels, challenges, achievements, and community events, such as competitions for the fastest completion times.

## 8. Meta-Game

The meta-game will focus on encouraging replayability through achievements, unlockable vehicles, and personal records.

Players could also attempt to improve their completion times or complete levels under additional restrictions, such as avoiding all damage.

## 9. Analysis of Risks and Opportunities

**Risks:**

- The niche market may have limited demand.
    
- The game's war setting may discourage some players.
    
- Limited development resources may restrict the game's scope.
    

**Opportunities:**

- Relatively little direct competition.
    
- A civilian perspective that differentiates the game from military-focused titles.
    
- Replayability through time challenges and achievements.
    
- Potential expansion to mobile platforms.
    

## 10. Summary and Recommendations

Our research suggests that the game has an opportunity to distinguish itself through its civilian perspective and combination of driving and wartime survival.

However, the limited market data makes commercial success uncertain.

We recommend developing a small playable prototype, testing it with potential players, and using their feedback to refine the gameplay before committing to a larger release.
