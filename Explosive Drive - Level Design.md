## Player Movement and Gameplay Overview

_Explosive Drive!_ is a fast-paced, single-player driving game in which the player must reach a destination while avoiding missiles, drones, traffic, and other obstacles. The game is designed around short runs, quick reactions, and learning how to navigate increasingly dangerous roads.

The player controls a vehicle and must steer to avoid hazards while continuing toward the destination. A progress indicator or minimap shows how far remains. The objective is to finish the route without being destroyed, preferably as quickly as possible.

Each level introduces a new threat before combining it with previously learned mechanics. Obstacles should be telegraphed clearly enough that the player can react, and failure should make the next attempt feel more informed rather than random.

**Example scenario:** The player is approaching a slow vehicle when a missile warning appears in the lane ahead. They must decide whether to brake, change lanes immediately, or wait for a safer opening. Changing lanes too quickly could put them in the path of another obstacle.

---

## Level 0 — Driving School (Tutorial)

**Difficulty:** Tutorial  
**Environment:** Empty training road or quiet outskirts

The player begins on a simple, mostly empty road with generous space to learn the controls. Instructions appear one at a time, followed by a safe opportunity to practice.

The player will learn how to:

- Steer and control the vehicle.
- Avoid stationary road obstacles.
- Recognize visual warnings for incoming threats.
- Use the vehicle's defensive or evasive ability, if implemented.
- Read the destination progress indicator or minimap.

The final tutorial section introduces one slow, clearly telegraphed missile that the player can dodge without other hazards competing for attention.

**Main challenge:** Learn to control the car and react to a single threat.

**Completion condition:** Reach the tutorial destination.

## Level 1 — Dangerous Highway (Easy)

**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Environment:** Open highway

The player enters a highway with moving civilian traffic and occasional incoming missiles. The road is wide enough to offer several safe routes around hazards.

This level introduces:

- Moving vehicles that act as obstacles.
- Missiles with clear impact indicators.
- Situations where a safe lane becomes temporarily blocked.
- Basic choices between speed and caution.

**Example scenario:** A truck blocks part of the road while a missile targets the player's current position. The player must steer into a free lane without colliding with passing traffic.

**Main challenge:** Dodge missiles while navigating traffic.

**Completion condition:** Reach the end of the highway without losing all health (or without taking a hit, if using a one-hit failure rule).

---

## Level 2 — Under Fire (Medium)

**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Environment:** Industrial district and damaged roads

The route becomes narrower and more unpredictable. Enemy drones appear and pressure the player while road hazards restrict available movement.

This level introduces:

- Drones that follow the player or attack from predictable positions.
- Damaged road sections, debris, or temporary blockages.
- Overlapping missile and drone attack patterns.
- Brief safe stretches that let the player recover and plan.

**Example scenario:** A drone approaches from the side while a missile targets the middle of the road. Debris blocks one escape route, forcing the player to identify the remaining safe path quickly.

**Main challenge:** Track multiple moving threats and choose safe routes under pressure.

**Completion condition:** Escape the industrial district and reach the checkpoint.

---

## Level 3 — Final Escape (Hard)

**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Environment:** Evacuation route through a heavily attacked area

The final level combines every previously introduced obstacle in a longer, more intense escape. It should test the player's understanding of warning signs, evasive movement, and road positioning rather than introducing many entirely new rules.

This level includes:

- Dense but readable traffic patterns.
- Frequent missile warnings with varied timing.
- Drones that force the player to reposition.
- Narrow passages and temporary road blockages.
- A final high-pressure stretch before reaching safety.

**Example scenario:** As the player approaches the destination, several missiles begin targeting different lanes while a drone closes in from behind. The player must navigate through a shifting safe corridor and avoid panicking into an obstacle.

**Main challenge:** Survive combinations of familiar threats while maintaining progress toward the destination.

**Completion condition:** Reach the evacuation point before the vehicle is destroyed.
